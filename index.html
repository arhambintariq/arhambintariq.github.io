Arham Bin Tariq

I am a graduate student in Robotics, Cognition, and AI at the Technical University of Munich. My research interests lie broadly in Machine Learning, and more specifically in probabilistic and robust learning methods, as well as efficient machine learning methods for resource-constrained devices. Until recently, I worked as a Research Assistant at Fraunhofer EMFT in the ML-Enhanced Sensors Group, where I conducted research on machine learning methods for time-series data and their implementation on embedded devices. I hold a Bachelor’s Degree in Electrical Engineering and Information Technology from Ravensburg-Weingarten University of Applied Sciences. When I am not glued to my computer, I am usually playing tennis, video games, or the piano.
